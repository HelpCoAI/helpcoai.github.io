Questions

Good to know

Do I have to pay anything upfront?

On 12-month plans: $0 setup, and you pay nothing until your AI is built, connected, and actually working. Prefer month-to-month? That's fine too, with a one-time setup fee that reflects the real build work.

How much does an AI employee cost?

Everything is a la carte with public prices: Essentials run $99/mo, revenue drivers $199/mo, SEO + AI Search is $399/mo, and the all-in AI-Smart Website is $999/mo including the site, Lead Catcher, Auto-Booking, CRM, and SEO. 10% off 3+ tools, 15% off 5+, 20% off 8+. See the full menu.

Is this an answering service?

It works like a 24/7 AI answering service - but instead of just taking messages, your AI receptionist answers questions, qualifies callers, and books the job straight onto your calendar. It's a virtual receptionist alternative starting at $99/mo.

What happens after I book the free demo?

We talk for about 15 minutes about where you're losing customers and time. Then I show you a live AI demo built with your business name on it. If you like it, I build the real thing. If not, no hard feelings - it costs you nothing.

Do I need to be techy?

Not at all. I handle 100% of the setup and maintenance. You just take the extra calls and bookings.

Will the AI sound robotic to my customers?

No. It is trained on your business, your hours, your services, and your tone. Most callers cannot tell, and there is always a path to reach a human.

How fast can I go live?

Usually within a few days of our call. Simple setups can be same week.

Is texting my customers legal?

Yes. I set everything up compliantly, with proper registration, opt-outs, and quiet hours. That is part of the service.

What if I want to cancel?

Month-to-month plans cancel anytime. 12-month plans run their term, then renew month to month. Either way you keep your data, your phone number, and your reviews when you go.
