Pricing FAQ

Is there really no setup fee?

On 12-month plans, correct: $0 setup and you pay nothing until your AI is live and working. On month-to-month, we charge a one-time setup fee, because real configuration work happens in week one either way.

What happens if I go over my Front Desk minutes?

Additional minutes are $0.35 each, billed transparently on your monthly report. If you're consistently over, that usually means the AI is answering a lot of calls, which is a good problem, and we'll suggest the plan that makes it cheapest for you.

How do the discounts work?

Three or more paid tools: 10% off everything. Five or more: 15% off. Eight or more: 20% off. Discounts apply automatically; you never have to ask.

Can I cancel anytime?

Month-to-month plans, yes, anytime. 12-month plans run their term, then renew month to month. Either way you keep your data, your phone number, and your reviews when you go.
