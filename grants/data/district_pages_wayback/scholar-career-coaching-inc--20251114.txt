At Scholar Career Coaching, we are dedicated to uplifting English Language Learners and First-Generation Scholars through mentorship and scholarship opportunities. Your involvement as a mentor, volunteer, sponsor, funding partner, or individual donor can truly make a difference in shaping our scholars&#8217; futures. Please reach out to us to get involved and be a part of this impactful journey! Executive Director We Coach, Motivate, and Support high potential scholars in Title I high schools in South Florida through mentoring services and scholarships. Who We Are We provide college prep services, introduce scholars to diverse career paths, award scholarships, and offer mentorship opportunities with passionate mentors who share similar academic and career interests. What We Do We have an incredible volunteer team and are continuously seeking talented individuals with diverse backgrounds, skills, and expertise to join Scholar Career Coaching, whether it be for short-term or long-term commitments. Get Involved Looking to Make a Difference? Help shape the future of aspiring scholars by sharing your knowledge and guidance. Whether you&#8217;re an experienced professional or an expert in your field, your contributions can make a meaningful difference in the lives of students. Join us in fostering growth, learning, and success!
