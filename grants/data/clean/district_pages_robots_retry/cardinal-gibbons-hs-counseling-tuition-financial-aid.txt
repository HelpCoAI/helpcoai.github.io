Includes Graduation Fees): $16,650 All families are required to set up a FACTS Tuition Management Account complete with valid payment/credit card information. It is the policy of the Archdiocese of Miami that all families complete a Tuition Agreement with the school. This document will be provided at registration. The first tuition payment for the academic year is due on or before July 15th, and all payments must be paid through FACTS. Payment plans include 10 monthly installments, quarterly, bi-annually, or in full. Re-registration is collected in January. Please contact the Bursar at bost@cghsfl.org or 954-491-2900 ext. 106, if you have questions or need assistance. Financial Aid Options Option 1: Tuition Assistance Option 2: Parishioners Tuition Reduction Option 3: Sibling Discount Option 1: Tuition Assistance State Sponsored Tuition Assistance Families may apply for State Funded Scholarships through the following: 1. Step-Up for Students (SUFS) www.stepupforstudents.org 2. Florida Private School Scholarship (AAA) www.aaascholarships.org Students are only eligible to receive funding from either SUFS or AAA, not both. Please check SUFS and AAA websites for application deadlines and interest lists. Parents/guardians are responsible for notifying Cardinal Gibbons of any award received. Please provide a screenshot of the student&rsquo;s name and Award ID to the Bursar at bost@cghsfl.org. Cardinal Gibbons Tuition Assistance Families may also apply now for additional need-based financial aid through Cardinal Gibbons Tuition Assistance Program, FACTS Grant and Aid (FACTS) http://online.factsmgt.com/signin/3TNs1 . Families will be considered for FACTS only if they have completed the following steps: Registered (submitted the registration fee) Created a FACTS Tuition Management Account, including selecting a payment plan and providing valid payment information in the tuition agreement. Applied to a State Program (SUFS or AAA) and submitted proof of application or award. It is the policy of the ADOM that all families complete a Tuition Agreement form with the school. This document will be provided at the time of registration. Option 2: Parishioners Tuition Reduction Cardinal Gibbons wants to support those who support the Church and our Catholic mission. Active parishioners of the Catholic Church who support their parish and regularly attend Mass on both Sunday and Holy Days of Obligation, are eligible for a discretionary tuition reduction in the amount of $1,500. To verify that parents/guardians have been supporting members of their parish for the past year, it is the responsibility of the parent/guardian to contact the parish to obtain proof of support by September 1st of the intended school year. Once active parishioner status has been established and verified, the discount will be reflected on the FACTS tuition account. *Parish discounts will not be applied to tuition accounts after September 1st of the active school year. Option 3: Sibling Discount Families with more than one student enrolled at Cardinal Gibbons for the designated school year are eligible for a multi-child reduction of $500 per child per school year.
