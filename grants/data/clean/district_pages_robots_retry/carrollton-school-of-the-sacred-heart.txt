The college counselors are experienced educators committed to the Goals and Criteria of Sacred Heart education and to helping each student grow in her awareness of her strengths, interests, and goals to make the best possible fit between herself and her post-secondary plans. The college counselors are committed to building strong partnerships with students, parents, counselors, and colleges. As soon as a student enters Grades 9-12 at Carrollton, the focus on college admission becomes integral to her life as a student. The decisions she makes regarding the classes she takes and the intellectual and extracurricular activities she pursues can impact her choices later. The college counselors utilize a three-pronged approach when working with the students&ndash; Know Yourself, Know the Process, and Know the Colleges. Partnership with the parents is an integral part of the journey and they are provided with relevant programming and opportunities to meet with the college counselors throughout the process. To Schedule a College Visit Carrollton looks forward to hosting college admission representatives to meet with interested juniors and seniors. We prefer in-person visits, which typically last between 30-45 minutes. Please use Naviance&rsquo;s Rep Visits to schedule your visit. Grade 8 students meet the college counselors and begin to familiarize themselves with the language used to speak about colleges. As Grade 9 students, they are introduced to concepts such as what it means to be in a college preparatory school, Carrollton&#39;s graduation requirements, the importance of building a robust academic and extracurricular record, and the factors colleges consider when admitting students during Freshman Success Week. Students adopt the mantra, &quot;Freshman Year Counts.&quot; Participate in Freshmen Success Week Presentation &ndash; Freshman Year Counts Take the Pre-ACT Test Engage in self-assessments through Naviance Attend Alumnae Career Day, Mentoring Program, and Career Presentations Curriculum Coffees for Parents Meet with an academic adviser for course selection for sophomore year Learn about summer enrichment and community service opportunities GRADE 10 In Grade 10, the focus shifts from introducing the factors that impact their college search to an action plan leading to deeper academic, service, and community engagements. Sophomores participate in an interactive college case studies exercise and begin to produce their resume. ​​​​​​Take the PSAT/NMSQT Test Participate in the JETS Tour on campus Attend Alumnae Career Day, Mentoring Program, and Career Presentations Curriculum Night for students and parents Create your high school resume Consider an externship and other summer enrichment activities Register for a Family/Student Q&amp;A Session with a college counselor Start test preparation over the summer Visit college campuses locally or when traveling GRADE 11 The formal program kicks into full gear in Grade 11. At this junction, students are assigned to one of three college counselors responsible for meeting with each student individually to learn of her aspirations, review her academic record, and generate a list of potential colleges and universities for the student to research. These initial meetings are followed by appointments with each family, allowing the parents to share their hopes, dreams, and realities with their daughters and their college counselor.
