Silver Knight Recognitions 2026 Nominees The Miami Herald Silver Knight Awards are among the most prestigious honors a high school student in Miami-Dade and Broward counties can receive. Established in 1959, these awards celebrate outstanding high school seniors who excel not only in academics but also demonstrate an exceptional and selfless commitment to community service. What Makes a Silver Knight? Silver Knight nominees are students who have consistently gone above and beyond to make a difference in their communities. While academic achievement (a minimum unweighted 3.2 GPA is required) is important, the core of the Silver Knight program lies in a student's dedication to service. This isn't just about accumulating volunteer hours; it's about initiating impactful projects, demonstrating leadership, and using unique talents to address real-world needs. Our School's Commitment Hialeah Gardens High School is incredibly proud to participate in the Miami Herald Silver Knight Awards. We believe this program perfectly aligns with our values of fostering both academic excellence and a strong sense of civic responsibility. Each year, we encourage our eligible seniors to consider this incredible opportunity to showcase their dedication and impact. Our faculty and staff are committed to supporting students through the nomination process, helping them articulate their service journeys and academic achievements. Categories of Recognition The Silver Knight Awards recognize students across 15 diverse categories, ensuring that a wide range of talents and contributions are celebrated. These categories include: Art, Athletics, Business, Digital &amp; Interactive Media, Drama, English &amp; Literature, General Scholarship, Journalism, Mathematics, Music &amp; Dance, Science, Social Science, Speech, Vocational Technical, and World Languages. Beyond the Award Being nominated for a Silver Knight Award is an honor in itself, a testament to years of hard work, dedication, and compassion. Winners and honorable mentions receive scholarships, but the true value lies in the recognition of their profound impact and the inspiration they provide to others. The Miami Herald Silver Knight Awards exemplify the power of young people to shape a better future for their communities.
