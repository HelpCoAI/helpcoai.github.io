Deadline to Apply – June 1st Only applicants who are not related to a current member of the Kiwanis Club of Jupiter -Tequesta can apply The Kiwanis Club of Jupiter- Tequesta awards scholarships to qualified applicants selected by the Scholarship Committee. Our purpose is to encourage young people who might not otherwise have an opportunity to pursue a college education. The awards are made annually. Completed applications must be postmarked by June 1st. 1. The applicant must be a resident of the Jupiter-Tequesta vicinity and live in an area bounded by Donald Ross Road on the south, Pratt-Whitney Road (zip code 33478) on the west, County Line Road (zip code 33469) on the north, and the Atlantic Ocean on the east. 2. The applicant must be a high s chool senior residing within the Jupiter-Tequesta area as defined above. 3. Additional consideration will be given to a member of Key Club or participant in community service. 4. Scoring: a. Personal Data 20 points b. Community Involvement 15 points c. SAT Score & Transcript 20 points d. Essay “Why I Need This Scholarship” 20 points (200 words or less) e.
