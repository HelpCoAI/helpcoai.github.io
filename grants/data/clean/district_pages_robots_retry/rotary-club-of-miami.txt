Rodriguez Welcome to the oldest Rotary Club in south Florida, we just turned 109 years young this year. Visit our club to connect with top Rotarians in South Florida. My vision for my presidential term can best be summarized by the word alignment: alignment with the Rotary International vision and priorities, alignment with our 4-way test, alignment with one another about who we are and who we want to be and alignment about how we will work together, as People of Action, through the 5 Avenues of service. Join us on this journey because together, we see a world where people unite and take action to create lasting change across the globe, in our communities and in ourselves.
