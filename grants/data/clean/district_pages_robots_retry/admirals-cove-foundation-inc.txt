Inspiring Generosity. Admirals Cove Foundation exists to improve lives in our northern Palm Beach County and to strengthen our own community through our shared culture of giving. Through our annual Season of Giving campaign, the Foundation&#8217;s efforts are focused exclusively in the areas of education, health and social services. Each year, through our Season of Giving campaign, we focus our support where it matters most: education, health, and social services. These pillars guide our funding decisions and our commitment to building a healthier, more resilient region. Our mission reflects something greater, a vibrant tradition of philanthropy that mirrors the exceptional quality of life we enjoy at Admirals Cove. Together, we are Admirals Cove Foundation. Our Focus Northern Palm Beach County Education We support nonprofit organizations, public and charter school programs and initiatives that promote and improve access to education for people of all ages. Health We support projects and programs of nonprofit organizations committed to improving the physical or mental wellness of children and adults. Social Services We support nonprofit organizations that improve the lives of families, children and individuals including veterans, who are in need of food, shelter, and assistance.
