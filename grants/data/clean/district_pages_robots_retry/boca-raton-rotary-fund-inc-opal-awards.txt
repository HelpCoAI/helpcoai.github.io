Proceeds from the OPAL Awards support the Rotary Club of Boca Raton's Scholarship Programs, developed in partnership with Florida Atlantic University and Palm Beach State College. The 29th Annual OPAL Awards will be held on Saturday, February 6, 2027, at Boca West Country Club. We are thrilled to recognize this year’s OPAL Honorees: Camilla &amp; Mike Goodwin, Jillian &amp; Adam Hasner, Zoe Lanham, Arline &amp; John McNally, and RCBR Rotarian Honoree John Mollica.
