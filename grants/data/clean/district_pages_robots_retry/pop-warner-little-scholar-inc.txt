In 2017, we began a 5-year stint in Pop Warner. The Warriors joined the American Alliance Youth Football, (AAYF) in 2023, as a founding member, along with other AYFL pioneer members. The main goal of our program is to develop character and confidence in children through competition in Football, Cheerleading & Track by fostering the life lessons which are inherent in these sports. We also place a high emphasis on academic performance and community involvement. FOOTBALL: Divisions range from 5 to 14 years old. Our conditioning camp program is included for registered participants, which is scheduled to commence in May. A Football Camp is also offered during the summer to compliment the Football Program. Regular season practices start during the summer. Competitive games take place each week, beginning in mid-August through October. All age divisions are scheduled to play on Saturdays with the youngest division first, immediately followed by the next age group, until all games are complete. Many of the games will be played on the Home Field (Tequesta Trace Park), while other games will be scheduled ‘away,’ at various parks in Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach Counties. Qualifying Football Teams will advance to the playoffs until a team is crowned Super Bowl Champion. The League Super Bowl Champions will continue to play in Regional / State Competitions during November with the opportunity to participate in a National Championship tournament in early December. CHEERLEADING: Divisions range from 5 to 15 years old. We have a summer conditioning camp, which is included for registered participants, scheduled to commence in June. Teams will start to take shape after beginning of the regular season on August 1st. The regular season will run from August through October on the sidelines of the Football games. Near the end of the regular season, Cheer Teams will compete in a local competition. Qualifying teams may continue to participate in Regional and National Cheer Competitions, which take place in November and December. TRACK: Divisions range from 5 to 18 years old for Boys & Girls. Regular season practices are from January to May. Participants must become members of AAU under Weston Warriors. Local Track meets are available for athletes (not required) on Saturdays beginning in February culminating with the Local Championship Meet in May. Qualifying athletes will have the opportunity to continue regionally & nationally with the National Championships being held in August. WESTON WARRIORS promotes a strong family atmosphere. We stress the importance of good sportsmanship amongst all of our members. We encourage all parents to support our players and cheerleaders by getting involved, volunteering, and attending our games and competitions. We look forward to welcoming you to the Warriors Family!
