It was coined from a Native American expression Nunc Kee-wanis . The Florida District is comprised of more than 240 clubs and over 7,500 Kiwanians within Florida. Florida is one of the largest and most successful districts in Kiwanis International. Young Children Priority One (YCPO) is an ongoing community service program of Kiwanis International that addresses the needs of early childhood (pregnancy to age five). The early emotional, physical, and social development of young children directly affects their overall growth and will ultimately become an influential factor in the adult they become.
