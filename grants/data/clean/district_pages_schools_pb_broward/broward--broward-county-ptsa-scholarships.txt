Our scholarship program is built on a simple promise: investing in students creates stronger futures for our entire community. 📌 Scholarship Information 🎓 Visit the scholarships page for the latest updates on eligibility, application details, important dates, and announcements. Applicants must be high school seniors and student members of a Broward County PTA or PTSA local unit. Renee Grutman Community Service Scholarship This scholarship recognizes graduating high school seniors who have demonstrated exceptional and sustained commitment to community service, volunteerism, and leadership, reflecting a strong dedication to making a meaningful impact beyond the classroom. APPLY HERE Barbara Walker General Scholarship This scholarship supports graduating high school seniors who demonstrate academic effort, character, and involvement and plan to continue their education at an accredited college or university with the goal of obtaining an associate or bachelor’s degree. APPLY HERE Career &amp; Technical Education (CTE)/Skilled Trades Scholarship This scholarship supports graduating high school seniors who plan to pursue a career and technical education pathway, including vocational training, certification programs, apprenticeships, or technical degrees. It recognizes students who demonstrate commitment to developing practical skills, career readiness, and a clear plan for entering the workforce or a skilled profession.
