Wanting to make quality relationships with other women in your area? Ready to give back to your local community and reap the benefits of a happier healthier life? Look no further; the Fort Lauderdale Woman&#8217;s Club is your &#8220;one-stop shop&#8221; for many community volunteer opportunities! Join us as we put into action to our favorite charities and passion projects. Come share your passion and your willingness to serve the community while connecting and building relationship with other women in Fort Lauderdale. Make a recognizable difference in civic, educational, cultural and humanitarian needs in our community by giving your time, money, talent or goods. We truly are a diverse and multi-aged group, so there is bound to be someone with whom you will connect with. Above all, we have fun! Feel free to drop by our monthly meetings held at our historic beautiful clubhouse (located at 20 South Andrews Avenue in downtown Fort Lauderdale) on the 3rd Thursday of every month (except June and July). Visit our events calendar to see what we are up to this month. Invite a friend to join you in checking us out. Hope to see you soon! FIND OUT MORE RENT OUR CLUBHOUSE RENT OUR CLUBHOUSE The Fort Lauderdale Woman’s Club is located on the southeast corner of Broward Boulevard and Andrews Avenue at the very heart of downtown Fort Lauderdale. It has a large main room and a smaller anteroom, along with a kitchen and restrooms. Conveniently located across from the city parking garage. The Fort Lauderdale Woman&#8217;s Club in conjunction with the William G. Pomeroy Foundation and the National Collaboration of Women&#8217;s Historic Sites invite you to a Dedication Ceremony for the National Votes for Women Trail honoring Ivy Stranahan . She is considered the Mother of the City of Fort Lauderdale, former club President 1913-1916, and a strong suffragist! She and her husband Frank donated the land where our clubhouse is located. Come and hear the little-known and well-known historical facts about both our club and Ivy Stranahan from the Fort Lauderdale Historical Society and the League of Women Voters. Join us for a champagne reception at 5:30 p.m. The dedication will begin at 6:15 p.m. Attendance is free for this historical dedication. RSVP is politely requested.
