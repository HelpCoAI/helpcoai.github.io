IRVIN STERN FAMILY IRVIN STERN FAMILY HOME GUIDELINES GRANTEES SPOTLIGHTS Contact Us HOME GUIDELINES GRANTEES SPOTLIGHTS Contact Us IRVIN STERN FAMILY ORG Scroll THE IRVIN STERN FAMILY FOUNDATION A private charitable trust Irvin Stern was a Chicago businessman who established a small charitable trust in 1957. At the time of his death, he left the administration of the Foundation to family members and charged them with helping charities and organizations that benefit social service organizations and enhance the welfare of Jewish communities. Today, the Irvin Stern Family Foundation is guided by family members who serve as Trustees. They are committed to supporting innovative grass roots human service and civic organizations, religious and secular, who aid the under-served and disadvantaged.
