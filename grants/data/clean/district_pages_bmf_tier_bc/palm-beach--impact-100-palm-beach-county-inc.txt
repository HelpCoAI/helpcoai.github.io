JOIN OR RENEW NOW Mission Statement Impact 100 Palm Beach County connects, engages, and inspires women to improve our community by collectively funding multiple $100,000 grants to nonprofits that implement high-impact initiatives in southern Palm Beach County. Vision Statement A thriving community with fully funded local nonprofit programs supported by the collective giving of women philanthropists. Core Values Compassion We believe in compassion as a guiding light; a light that leads our women members to give from the heart to local nonprofit organizations who, in turn, light the way in our community for those in need. Empowerment We empower women by educating our members in the power of effective philanthropy and collaboration through the collective, compassionate action of "giving as one." Vision We are visionary women philanthropists who effectively serve our community by providing our nonprofit partners with the means to envision, create, and implement dream projects that will transform our community. Transformation We are women-led philanthropists who ignite the positive power of transformation within every member, nonprofit organization, and community we touch. Transparency We are committed to the transparent stewardship of our members&#x2019; contributions by ensuring each grant&#x2019;s timely implementation and by providing clear, accurate reports of funds spent and results achieved.
