More information can be found at: www.ayes.org/Students-Parents/Scholarships/JFS-Scholarships.aspx 2014 Ford /AAA Auto Skills Student Florida State Finals Ricardo J. Martinez and Kumar Y. Shah from Robert Morgan Educational Center’s – AYES Automotive Service Technology program placed Third out of the top ten qualifying Florida schools invited to compete at the 2014 Ford /AAA Auto Skills Student Florida State Finals: Hands-On Repair Competition which was held at Seminole State College in Sanford Florida on Wednesday, May 14th 2014. The students were required to diagnose and make repairs within one and a half hours to one of ten identically bugged 2014 Ford Fiesta SE equipped with 1.6L 4 cylinder, Automatic 6 speed transmission, Air conditioner, Power windows with door locks, AM/FM/CD radio, Speed control systems and Advancetrac stability control and more! For their eforts the students have received $16,500.00 in scholarships, over $350.00 in tools from NAPA, Snap-On, Matco and Bosch Tool companies plus an AAA Roadside Services membership for their accomplishment.
