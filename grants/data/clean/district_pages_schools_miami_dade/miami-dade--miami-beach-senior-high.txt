Alumni Site Contact MBSH Faculty and Staff Directory PTSA PTSA Home Join PTSA Order Uniforms Explore MDCPS College Assistance Program (CAP) The College Assistance Program, popularly known as CAP, provides college information to the students and families of Miami Beach Senior High. As the CAP Advisor, students and parents are welcome to make appointments to discuss your child’s college pathway. You can schedule an appointment with the CAP Advisor using your student&#8217;s SCOIR account; this is our district&#8217;s college and career readiness platform. Please see this LINK on how to schedule an appointment. CAP Scholarship Bulletin The CAP Scholarship Bulletin provides information and links to over 300 postsecondary scholarship opportunities. Students are encouraged to subscribe to scholarship search engines to access additional scholarship opportunities as this bulletin is not an exhaustive list.
