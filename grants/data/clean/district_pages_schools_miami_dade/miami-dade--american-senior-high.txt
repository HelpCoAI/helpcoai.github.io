At ASHS, the program guides students from their 9 th grade Research classes to their 12 th grade CAP class. From exploring college, technical college, military and work programs, to assisting with testing, to advising on financial aid and scholarship opportunities, our Patriots leave ASHS ready to take the next steps in their post-secondary journey with confidence.
