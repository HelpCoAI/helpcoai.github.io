The College Assistance Program (CAP) is an advisory program initiated by Miami-Dade County Public Schools to help students in the process of researching, exploring, and applying to colleges. At JMMAST, the program guides students from their 9 th grade Research classes to their 12 th grade CAP class, where every senior is enrolled in a course with our CAP Advisor to facilitate every step in the journey to college. From exploring college options, to assisting with testing, to advising on financial aid and scholarship opportunities, our Silver Knights leave JMMAST ready to take the next steps in their post-secondary journey with confidence.
