Education has multiple avenues, many that have yet to be explored by current graduating high school seniors. This scholarship is given on the premise that the Sprouting Scholar has yet to reach their peak academic potential or greatest educational achievement. This award is a reminder that there is a community of people hoping to see you sprout into a harvest of knowledge for others to reap.
