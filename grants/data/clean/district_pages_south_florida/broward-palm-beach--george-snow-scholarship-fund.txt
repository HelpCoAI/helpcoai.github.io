Nursing Scholarship Criteria Nursing Undergraduate and Graduate Eligibility Criteria: To qualify, you must meet all the following criteria Preference given to residents of Palm Beach County. You must exhibit financial need. You must be accepted into a nursing program by May 1, 2026. You must be enrolled for either Summer 2026 or Fall 2026 classes. Must maintain a minimum GPA based on your program&#8217;s criteria: You must be enrolled in an accredited nursing program in Florida (CCNE and/or ACEN), or an online program approved for Florida licensure. Graduate Nursing scholarships require applicants to hold of Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN) or to be enrolled in an accredited RN-to-MSN or equivalent bridge program. Behavioral/Mental Health Scholarship Criteria Behavioral Health/Mental Health Undergraduate and Graduate Eligibility Criteria: To qualify, you must meet all the following criteria: Preference given to residents of Palm Beach County. You must exhibit financial need. You must be enrolled at least for Summer 2026 or Fall 2026 classes Must maintain a minimum GPA based on your program&#8217;s criteria. Undergraduate Eligibility: Students must be pursuing a bachelor&#8217;s degree in a major that serves as a pathway into a graduate program required for professional licensure in behavioral or mental health. You must be a Junior or Senior. Eligible majors include: Social Work Psychology Human Services Child and Family Studies Psychiatric Nursing (accredited &gt; PMHNP/ARNP) Individuals who already hold credentials such as Community Health Worker (CHW), Certified Recovery Peer Specialist (CRPS), Certified Nursing Assistants (CNA), or Behavioral Health Technician (BHT) are strongly encouraged to apply as they continue advancing their education and career in the behavioral health and nursing field. Graduate Eligibility: Student must be pursuing a master’s or doctoral degree in an accredited program (On-campus, hybrid, or online) that leads to professional licensure in mental or behavioral health. Eligible programs include (but are not limited to): Social Work (CSWE-accredited MSW&gt;LCSW) Counseling / Clinical Mental Health Counseling (CACREP-accredited &gt; LMHC). Marriage and Family Therapy (COAMFTE-accredited&gt; LMFT) Psychology (APA-accredited doctoral &gt; Licensed Psychologist) Applied Behavior Analysis (ABAI-verified &gt; BCBA) Psychiatric Nursing (accredited &gt; PMHNP/ARNP). Doctor of Social Work (DSW) programs will also be considered if the applicant already holds a CSWE-accredited MSW. School/Degree Eligibility Students must be enrolled in an accredited mental or behavioral health program in Florida or online that meets the educational requirements for licensure under the Florida Board of Clinical Social Work, Marriage and Family Therapy, and Mental Health Counseling or related licensing boards. The George Snow Scholarship Fund advertises the availability of awards through a variety of media and promotes each scholarship with high school scholarship coordinators and guidance counselors. Applications The online application becomes available on our website on November 1 st each year. The deadline for completion is February 1 st of the following year. Once completed, the applications are processed and delivered to the Scholarship Selection Committee. The George Snow Scholarship Fund has an outstanding group of community volunteers who sit on the Selection Committee to review all applications. The Scholarship Selection Committee is comprised of professionals; including educators, businessmen and women, and individuals who are considered experts in certain areas. Most committee members have served many years and have a great deal of experience in our process. All share a passion for helping others. Each selection committee member is assigned a certain amount of applications which are reviewed and ranked in the appropriate areas. The rankings are then submitted to our office, where they are entered into a program that compiles all the rankings and allows us to ensure that every applicant is being equally assessed. Based on the rank from that scoring, the candidate is either declined or invited to interview. Interviews Students who are selected to interview are contacted via email to schedule an interview meeting. For the interview, the selection committee is divided into teams of four. Each team interviews all the candidates assigned to their team and grades each individual candidate according to the results of their interview. Financial Need Analysis During this process, a separate team gathers necessary information such as high school transcripts, student aid reports, and family tax returns that are used to substantiate information provided on the candidates application. From here a Financial Need Analysis is performed. Once all information is obtained, we then take our selection committee grades and merge them with the established financial need to assign scholarships that are based on need and who best meets the established criteria.
