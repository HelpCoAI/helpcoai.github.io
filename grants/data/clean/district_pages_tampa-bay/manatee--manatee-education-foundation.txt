Joan Tomeo Perseverance Scholarship Name of Scholarship: Joan Tomeo Perseverance Scholarship About: The Joan Tomeo Perseverance Scholarship is awarded to a young lady who has faced significant life challenges, including teen parenthood, and has demonstrated resilience, determination, and commitment to continuing their education. This scholarship honors Joan&#8217;s dedication to helping young women stay in school and build a brighter future. Deadline: May 4, 2026 Area of Study: Various Eligibility: The recipient is a graduating senior from a public school in Manatee County. This student is committed to furthering their education, whether at an accredited four-year college or university, a two-year college, or a technical school, beginning in the 2026-2027 academic year. They demonstrate remarkable responsibility by managing the demands of both school and parenting. Their commitment to personal growth reflects their strength of character, perseverance, and heartfelt determination to create a stable and promising future for themselves and those they care for. The scholarship funds are intended to ease their journey, covering tuition, necessary educational fees, and/or other essential course-related expenses, so they can focus on learning and building a better life for their family.
