OUR CORE PROGRAMS The Elevate Prize Awarded annually, the Elevate Prize recognizes ten best-in-class social impact leaders driving transformative change across issue areas around the world. It’s designed to amplify their story and, in turn, widen their reach and maximize their impact. elevate studios Elevate Studios is creating an ecosystem for stories that don’t just entertain but inspire. This strategic arm elevates the voices of changemakers to make doing good not just famous, but universal and baked into the fabric of our every day. The elevate prize GET LOUD AWARD The Elevate Prize GET LOUD Award is a monthly $25,000 grant created to fuel community-based organizations on the frontlines of society’s most pressing challenges. The Catalyst AWARD The Elevate Prize Catalyst Award recognizes prominent individuals or groups who use their influence to drive social action, providing $250,000 in unrestricted funding to deepen their impact and catalyze change.
