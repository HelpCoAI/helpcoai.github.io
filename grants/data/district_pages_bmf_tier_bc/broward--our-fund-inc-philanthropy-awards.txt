SOUTH FLORIDA LGBTQ PHILANTHROPY AWARDS In 2019, The Our Fund Foundation launched its signature philanthropist recognition program, The South Florida LGBTQ Philanthropy Awards, which recognizes those individuals who give of their time, talent, and treasure and demonstrate a significant philanthropic impact on South Florida’s LGBTQ community. Annual awardees are recognized for financial commitment (Philanthropist of the Year), excellence in volunteerism (Commitment to a Cause), and emerging philanthropy (Next Generation of Philanthropy). In addition, the Dick Schwarz Lifetime Achievement Award, named after and emblematic of The Foundation’s co-founder, Dick Schwarz, recognizes prodigious achievements in giving back to the community. Nominations for awards are solicited from agencies in The Foundation’s network of LGBTQ-serving nonprofits and are accepted throughout the year.
