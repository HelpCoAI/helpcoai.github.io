PELS TZEDEK TIRDOF FOUNDATION Repairing the World by Advancing Jewish Values OUR MISSION The Mission of the PELS Tzedek Tirdof is to perpetuate a just and prosperous society by supporting organizations that advance Jewish values, repair our world, promote justice, and enrich our educational and cultural experiences. We are committed to developing young Jewish leaders, and to supporting initiatives that promote justice, sustainability, and conservation through education and the arts.
