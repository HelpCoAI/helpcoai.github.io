Simon Lakeview Apartments Receives Spirit of Partnership Award 3 years ago Blog , Our News 2023 On November 3, 2023, Clyde F. Simon Lakeview I and II Apartments was recognized by the Cornell Cooperative Extension of Steuben County as recipient of the Spirit of Partnership Award for providing exceptional service. The staff also received a proclamation of gratitude from Senator Tom O’Mara, Assemblyman Philip Palmesano, Assemblywoman Marjorie Byrnes, and Assemblyman Joseph Giglio. The proclamation recognized their Spirit of Partnership for contributions to the success and vitality of the lives of their fellow citizens, For the last six months, the property has welcomed a weekly Veggie Van to the community. The van was provided through the Cornell Cooperative Extension in collaboration with the Office for the Aging, which provided Farmer’s Market coupons in the amount of $20 that could be used at the Veggie Van or any other produce market in the county. (L-R) Kelsey Monks, Senior Nutritionist for SNAP ED NY Southern Finger Lakes Region; Carey Spara, EHDOC Community Manager; and Lena Smith, EHDOC Service Coordinator. A six-week course of nutrition and grocery tips through SNAP-ED NY (from the Cornell Cooperative Extension) was also offered at Lakeview Apartments. Ten residents participated in the program learning about topics such as how to buy food on a budget, how to prepare food, and how to stay healthy. Each attendee received a $20 voucher for fruits and/or vegetables from local produce stands including the Veggie Van.
