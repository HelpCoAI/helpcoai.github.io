During the month of July 2026, Sweet Dream Makers See more about our impact THE NEED Nearly 7,500 beds were requested by families in need in 2025. Founded in 2012 in response to an overwhelming community need to get children into a bed of their own, Sweet Dream Makers delivers new beds, at no cost, to children and families sleeping on the floor, sharing beds, or sleeping in unsafe, unsanitary conditions. Learn more about what we do What we give A good night's sleep means more than a mattress. Having a safe, clean, and appropriate place to sleep is a critical part of long-term health and wellness with measurable educational benefits. Mattress, boxspring, &amp; mattress protector We provide healthy sleeping essentials to every family. A complete bedding set Children choose their favorite covers to foster pride and independence. Better education and grades Students read more and perform better in school when they are well rested. Strength and resilience Families are better prepared to face life after a good night's rest.
