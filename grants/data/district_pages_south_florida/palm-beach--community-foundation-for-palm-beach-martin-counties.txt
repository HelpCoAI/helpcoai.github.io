The Community Foundation is cheering you on! Our Application Process How It Works 1. Submit your application. 2. Foundation staff review and match you to scholarships. 3. Finalists may be invited to interview. 4. Scholarship committee recommends recipients. 5. Board approves awards. 6. Funds go directly to your school. Key Dates – 2026 Cycle Scholarship applications for the 2026 cycle are now closed. Current applicants should continue to monitor their email for updates about interviews and awards. We look forward to reopening applications next year. Feb. 2, 2026 Applications open Mar. 4, 2026 (5 p.m. ET) Deadline to apply Mar. 6, 2026 (5 p.m. ET) Recommendation letters due April 2026 Interviews for finalists Mid-May 2026 Awards announced June 19, 2026 Scholar Celebration July 2026 Scholarship payments sent to schools ---> Eligibility Some scholarships have additional requirements. Minimum 2.0 GPA Graduating high school senior in Palm Beach or Martin County Incoming student at a college, university, or trade/vocational school Demonstrated financial need and/or academic achievement Be a U.S. Citizen or Permanent Resident What You’ll Need Unofficial transcript Completed essays Letter(s) of recommendation U.S. Birth Certificate U.S. Passport (Book or Card) Certificate of Citizenship (Form N-560 or N-561) Certificate of Naturalization (Form N-550 or N-570) Employment Authorization Document (Form I-766) Permanent Resident Card (Form I-551; “Green Card”) Ready to Apply?
