The scholarship is named after María and Horacio Sierra and their family . The couple dedicated their careers to improving the lives of Miami&#39;s youth by teaching at Coral Park and coaching at Tamiami Park as well as with the Miami Mini-Canes softball organization. Coral Park is a Title I school with a majority-Hispanic student, teacher, and staff population.
