to CAP Corner Hello Knights! My name is Ms. Rosell , and I am honored to serve as your College Assistance Program (CAP) Advisor this school year. The journey to life after high school, whether it’s college, technical school, the military, or the workforce, can feel overwhelming, but you’re not in it alone. I’m here to guide you through every step of the process: from exploring your options and applying to colleges, to securing financial aid and celebrating your next chapter. Whether you’re a freshman just starting to explore, a junior preparing for testing, or a senior getting ready to submit applications, my door is always open . I offer one-on-one meetings, workshops, college visits, and tons of resources to make your transition smooth and successful. Need help or have questions? You can book an appointment using my online calendar or stop by during my walk-in hours . Let’s make this year a great one, full of opportunity, growth, and next steps toward your future!
