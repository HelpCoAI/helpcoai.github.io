The points of the compass direct Blue Scholars to achieve four guiding principles. Education Our true north is to provide hands-on curriculum on marine biology, marine ecology and watershed concepts. Research East of Miami lies Biscayne Bay where our students sample and record water quality data for citizen science. Impact To the west , participating groups perform a community service project to earn their trip and benefit the greater good. Opportunity Blue Scholars seeks to develop expedition partnerships south in the Caribbean to expand programming and impact.
