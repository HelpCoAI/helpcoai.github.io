The mission of the iTHINK Community Foundation is to improve the lives of children and families in the communities we serve. Our 501(c)3 non-profit organization, the iTHINK Community Foundation, believes that children are our future, and it is important to invest in their success now. That's why all proceeds from our events will be donated directly to the foundation and used to support local causes like scholarship awards and school supplies for children in need.
