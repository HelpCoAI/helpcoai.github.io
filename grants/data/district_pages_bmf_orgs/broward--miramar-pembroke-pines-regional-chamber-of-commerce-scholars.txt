 Scholarships | South Broward Foundation - South Broward Foundation About Scholarships Leadership Nonprofits Support Contact Scholarship Fund Scholarship Fund With its origins as a committee of the Miramar Pembroke Pines Regional Chamber of Commerce, the Chamber's Scholarship Fund has grown to be its own independent charitable foundation that raises over $20,000 annually. Funds are raised specifically for the support an enhancement of quality education in the cities of Miramar and Pembroke Pines, and this now extends to the Weston, Pembroke Park, and West Park areas. The Scholarship Committee raises funds to honor and award scholarships to students who exhibit outstanding contributions to their community and school, and who demonstrate academic excellence. Award amounts range from $500 to $3,000. Beginning with the 2025-2026 school year, funds will be allocated to each school and each school will select their recipients based on criteria provided by the Foundation board.
