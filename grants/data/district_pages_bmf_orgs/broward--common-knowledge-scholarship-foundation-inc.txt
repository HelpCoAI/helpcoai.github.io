From developing the nationally recognized FBI Safe Online Surfing program to partnering with university students on real-world projects, CKSF has continually evolved to meet the changing needs of students. Today, we focus on providing exceptional student experiences through internships, research, and applied AI skills—helping students build the confidence, leadership, and professional experience needed to succeed in college, careers, and life. Over the years, CKSF developed a unique scholarship model that rewarded students for what they knew about specific academic and educational topics, encouraging learning, curiosity, and achievement. Today, our primary scholarship initiative is the Last Mile Scholarship , which helps determined students cross the finish line on their path to graduation. Rather than focusing solely on academic performance, the Last Mile Scholarship supports students who have demonstrated perseverance, commitment, and the desire to complete their degree despite financial or personal challenges. At CKSF, we believe that talent and hard work should not be derailed by a final obstacle. The Last Mile Scholarship reflects our commitment to helping students reach one of life's most important milestones—earning a college degree and preparing for a successful future.
