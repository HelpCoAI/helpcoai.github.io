YES INSTITUTE Non-Profit Community Service - Instructions Non-Profit Service Awards - Application Soroptimist International Boca Raton/Deerfield annually seeks qualified applicants for its Non-Profit Community Service Awards. Applications must be received by March 31st of each year Soroptimist of Boca's mission includes providing grants to local non-profit organizations in need of financial support for specific projects. Projects directly benefitting women and girls are of particular interest to Soroptimist of Boca. Applications, or inquiries, may be mailed to Soroptimist of Boca Raton-Deerfield Beach, PO Box 657, Boca Raton FL 33429 or emailed to soroptimist4women@gmail.com , or Chairperson, Cynthia Cummings at 561-330-9166 or cicdelray@yahoo.com. Eligibility Rules: The following documents MUST accompany any application to be considered eligible: 1) Verification of non-profit status 2) Current list of Board of Directors and their affiliations 3) Most recent financial statement 4) Project budget 5) Supporting information about project 6) Documentation of need for the project 7) W-9 completed.
