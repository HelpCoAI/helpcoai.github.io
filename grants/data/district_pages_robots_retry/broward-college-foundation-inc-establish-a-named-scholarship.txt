Close preview HEAR Yanelle's STORY Each year, Broward College awards more than $2 million in student scholarships that have been funded through the generosity of many benefactors. These dollars allow the college to recruit and retain students. Establishing a named annual scholarship that will have an almost immediate impact on students requires a gift of $2,500 or more, payable at once or over a period of 12 months. Donors may designate whether their scholarship will be awarded based on academic merit and/or financial need. Additionally, a program of study may be specified. Scholarship criteria are intended to expedite those opportunities and the impact of each donor’s generosity; they must reflect the interests of the Broward College student body and not the private interests of the donor. For more information, download and review our Charitable Gift Acceptance Policies . To establish a named annual scholarship, call 954-201-7414 to schedule an appointment with one of our Major Gifts Officers.
