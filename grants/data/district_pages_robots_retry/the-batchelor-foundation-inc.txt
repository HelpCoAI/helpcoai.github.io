Established in 1990 by Mr. George E. Batchelor, founder and sole benefactor, the Batchelor Foundation makes primarily program grants subject to geographic restrictions. Only nonprofit organizations within the targeted geographic region which qualify for public charity status under section 501(c)(3) of the United States Internal Revenue Code and the Treasury Regulations thereunder are eligible for grant making consideration. As of November 15, 2023, the Batchelor Foundation will only accept grant applications from nonprofits that we have funded in the past. 2017-07-11 ©2026 The Batchelor Foundation, Inc.
