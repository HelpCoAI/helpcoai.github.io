Close preview HEAR STORY Broward College Memorial Endowed Scholarship, A Lasting Legacy Providing the gift of education through a contribution to the Broward College Memorial Endowed Scholarship is a way of keeping a loved one’s memory alive in perpetuity and creates a legacy for individuals who are being memorialized through educational opportunities the scholarship provides. Each and every gift to the Broward College Memorial Endowed Scholarship enhances and grows the annual impact of the scholarship by assisting Broward College students attain their dream of a higher education. The Broward College Memorial Endowed Scholarship was established with gifts from the estate of Arthur Foss, a long-time college professor and with leadership gifts in memory of Broward College employee, Shermone Mitchell, who died while employed at the College in 2018. When making a tribute gift, please specify the individual for whom you are making a gift in memory of. You can add a note when mailing in a check, or when making a gift online . Learn more about tribute gifts at Broward College. The memory of the following individuals lives on through the Broward College Memorial Endowed Scholarship: Arthur Foss Shermone Mitchell Dean Linda Wood Carlos Parra Sandra "Sandi" Pebley Donald L.
