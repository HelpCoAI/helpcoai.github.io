 2025-2026 Annual Award Winners - Junior League of Boca Raton Members Join Give Junior League of Boca Raton Search Menu 2025-2026 Annual Award Winners Every member of the Junior League of Boca Raton contributes their time, talent and effort to the our current community issues of non-profit support and child welfare. At the Junior League of Boca Raton May Dinner meeting, awards are given to members who have created, inspired and/or encouraged others throughout the year. Founders Award: Created in honor of the founding members of Junior League of Boca Raton, this award is presented to a Junior League of Boca Raton member in recognition of outstanding service to the League. The award is presented to an active member in good standing who has been a member of Junior League of Boca Raton for at least three years. The award recognizes the dedication, motivation and enthusiasm of a member who has earned her the respect and admiration of her colleagues. The Founders Award recognizes exemplary performance within the Junior League of Boca Raton over a period of time. The 2025 &#8211; 2026 Award Winners were Cyd Alderman, Alyson Baugh, Robin Deyo, Michelle Duffy, Greta Gillis, Nicole Mugavero, Lisa Mulhall, Diane Nestor, Kimberly Read, Heide Taylor, Kimberley Trombly-Burmeister, Karen Wadowicz, and Lisa Warren. Outstanding Service Award: This award is presented to a Junior League of Boca Raton member who has taken her active Junior League of Boca Raton involvement and training into the community to make a major contribution through volunteer service. The award winner must be an active or sustainer in good standing that has been a member of Junior League of Boca Raton for at least three years. The award may be given for outstanding achievement through Junior League of Boca Raton in the community or the award may be presented for outstanding service in the community over a period of time. Please note “community” is defined to include and extend beyond a Junior League of Boca Raton project. The 2025 &#8211; 2026 Winner was Francine Lang. Outstanding Sustainer Award: This award is presented to a sustaining member of Junior League of Boca Raton in good standing. The award recognizes a member’s dedication, motivation and enthusiasm and has earned the respect and admiration of her colleagues and will recognize exemplary performance within the Junior League of Boca Raton over a period of time. The 2025 &#8211; 2026 Award Winner was Jackie Reeves. The Torch Award: This award will be presented to an active or sustaining member of Junior League of Boca Raton in good standing. The Torch Award recognizes a member who is a true pioneer, who embraces innovation and creativity. This member willingly forges into new territory, breaks new ground or takes on something never before attempted. She maintains confidence and enthusiasm in the face of doubt, opposition or barriers. The award winner is someone who never says never, who does something unique and different and who achieves desired goals. The 2025 &#8211; 2026 Award Winners were Maria Bedoya &amp; Lauren Pomerantz. The Starburst Award: This award is presented to a Junior League of Boca Raton active member in good standing who puts forth the extra effort despite the number of contributions or commitments she is already doing for the Junior League of Boca Raton. The award recognizes the person you contact if you want something done, even though she is busy, and you know she will make time to do the job well. The award recognizes the ability to provide that extra “burst of energy” to help get the task at hand completed.
