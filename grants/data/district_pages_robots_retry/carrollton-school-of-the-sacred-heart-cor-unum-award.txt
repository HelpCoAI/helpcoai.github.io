Their unparalleled generosity, dedication, and service embody a spirit of selfless giving. These community leaders' extraordinary charity, strategic vision, and impactful contributions significantly enhance the school's ability to serve its community and fulfill its mission guided by the Goals and Criteria of Sacred Heart education. Their devotion to contributing time, talent, and treasure exemplifies extraordinary leadership, enabling Carrollton to fulfill its Sacred Heart mission as a school of Christ’s heart in Miami. Their support and selflessness have not only shaped the present success of our school but have also paved the way for our continued growth. Their commitment has been instrumental in establishing Carrollton School of the Sacred Heart as a distinguished institution, and we are profoundly grateful for the lasting impact they have made on our spiritual and educational journey.
