College Advising pause slideshow play slideshow &lt; &gt; The School Counseling Department at Cardinal Gibbons High School plays a vital role in advancing the school&rsquo;s mission of Catholic values, academic excellence, lifelong learning, and service by supporting each student&rsquo;s academic, personal, social, and spiritual development. Guided by compassion and integrity, counselors walk with students throughout all four years, helping them navigate challenges, make meaningful decisions, and grow into responsible, faith-filled individuals. In alignment with the school&rsquo;s vision of &quot;Elevating our Faith and Academics,&quot; counselors provide Christ-centered guidance lessons, one-on-one counseling, and faith-integrated academic planning. By nurturing each student&rsquo;s potential and affirming their God-given gifts, the department fosters a school culture that values both spiritual formation and academic growth, preparing students to serve with purpose in college, career, and life. Grounded in faith, our Counseling Team emphasizes well-being, self-advocacy, and spiritual growth. We remind students that high school is a journey, one where they can seek help, build confidence, and become the person God calls them to be.
