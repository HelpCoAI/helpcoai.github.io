# Scholarships that exist to buy backlinks

Companies create a $1,000 "scholarship", ask for an essay, and pitch universities
to link to it from a `.edu` financial-aid page. The award is a link-building
budget line. This is a recognised tactic with agencies openly selling it as a
service.

## What it means for us, measured rather than asserted

Screened all 137 enriched records for four signals: a commercial sponsor in an
unrelated industry (law firm, insurance, SEO agency, roofing), text asking
applicants to share or link, a small award with national scope, and a single
award given nationally.

**2 of 137 records (1.5%) carry any signal.** Both are national awards that reached
us second-hand through a district's aggregated list:

| award | sponsor | signal |
|---|---|---|
| Credit Worthy Scholarship Contest | OneMain Financial | small and national |
| Annual Hope Scholarship | The Law Office of Philips and Raaymakers | commercial sponsor |

## Why the rate is that low, and why it is the point

We do not accept submissions. Every record is crawled from a school counselor
page, a community foundation, or a service club that already administers the
award. Backlink-bait scholarships reach databases by being *submitted* to them,
which is the acquisition channel we do not have.

So the structural claim is available to us honestly: **an index built by crawling
local sources is close to immune to a problem that a submission-driven database
cannot avoid.**

## What we must NOT claim

We have no measurement of anyone else's rate. Fastweb lists over 1.5 million
opportunities and we cannot see inside it; asserting that some share of them are
link bait would be a number we invented. The honest form is a statement about our
own data, with the mechanism explained, and no comparison.

## Three ways to use it

1. **Screen and label, do not silently drop.** A OneMain scholarship is still real
   money a student can win. Flag the sponsor's commercial interest on the record
   rather than hiding the award.
2. **A guide page.** "How to tell a real scholarship from a marketing campaign"
   is a genuine student question, answerable from signals rather than opinion,
   and it targets a query the incumbents have no incentive to write.
3. **A provider rule for the free listing tool.** When sponsors can list
   themselves, this becomes the intake filter that stops us acquiring the problem
   the moment we open submissions.
