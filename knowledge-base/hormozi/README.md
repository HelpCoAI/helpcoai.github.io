# Hormozi Operating Knowledge Base

A working reference of the business frameworks popularized by Alex Hormozi, written
as original synthesis and applied to **HelpCo AI** — an AI front-desk / receptionist
product for local service businesses on Florida's Gulf Coast.

This is not a book summary service. It is a decision tool: each file ends with how the
framework applies to *this* business, so it can be used to make a call on pricing, an
offer, a channel, or a hire.

## Provenance and honest limitations

Read this before treating anything here as gospel.

**What this is built from.** My own knowledge of these frameworks, which are widely
documented and discussed publicly, plus web research to verify the structure of the
newest material. Frameworks, formulas, and methods are ideas — they are restated here
in original words with original examples.

**What this is *not* built from.**

1. **The books themselves.** I did not obtain, download, or ingest PDF copies of
   *$100M Offers*, *$100M Leads*, or *$100M Money Models*. They are commercial books
   still on sale; the copies circulating on file-sharing sites are pirated, and this
   knowledge base contains no text from them. Nothing here reproduces the books'
   actual wording, structure, examples, or exercises. If you want the source material,
   buy the books — at typical pricing the three of them cost less than one hour of most
   consultants, and the specific examples and scripts are the part I can't give you.

2. **The three videos you linked.** This environment's network policy blocks
   `youtube.com` outright (HTTP 403 at the egress proxy on every request). I also tried
   to identify them by video ID through web search so I could at least work from public
   discussion of their content — no result matched `Mst4hreQYl0`, `HxEQCHpZzHk`, or
   `HwmwyBgzj8c`. So those three videos are genuinely not in here. If you want them
   covered, paste the titles or transcripts into a session and I'll fold them in.

**Confidence levels.** *Offers* and *Leads* material (files 02–05) I know well and
state plainly. *Money Models* (file 06) is recent; the three-stage structure and the
core metrics are verified against public sources, but I've flagged the places where I'm
working from the shape of the argument rather than precise detail.

**The bias worth naming.** This body of work is built on high-margin, high-ticket,
service and info businesses, sold largely to an audience of entrepreneurs. It travels
well to a B2B software-and-service business selling to local operators — which is what
HelpCo is — but the aggressive guarantee and urgency tactics assume margins and a
customer psychology that don't always hold. Where a tactic would be a bad idea for a
recurring-subscription business selling to plumbers, the file says so rather than
repeating it.
